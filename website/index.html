My TouchBar. My Rules.

A free, open-source app that turns the MacBook Pro Touch Bar into exactly what you want it to be.

Version 0.28 Native on Apple Silicon & Intel macOS 12 or later Signed & notarized
Download 0.28 · DMG GitHub
brew install --cask mtmr

Make it yours

Describe your Touch Bar in a simple JSON preset — or build it visually in the customizer — and MTMR keeps it there.

Built-in widgets

Dock, music, weather, battery, CPU, network, currency, calendar, pomodoro, dark mode, Do Not Disturb and more.

Your own buttons

Run AppleScript or shell scripts, press keys, open URLs. Script output can update a button's title and icon live.

Per-app presets

Show different buttons for different apps with matchAppId, or hide MTMR entirely in apps you choose.

Gestures

Swipe with two, three or four fingers to change volume and brightness or run your own actions. Single, double, triple and long taps.

Visual customizer

New in 0.28: arrange items with a preview, edit their settings in an inspector, and save straight to your preset.

Haptic feedback

Feel every press through the trackpad, on Apple Silicon and Intel MacBooks alike.

What's new in 0.28
